## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:**
Vyom ERP is an industrial electrical panel manufacturing console, designed for plant supervisors and shop floor engineers scanning high-density data tables across 8-hour shifts. However, its current visual implementation falls into the "pitch-black obsidian void" trap (`#0b0f19` canvas, `#111827` surface). While meant to evoke a sci-fi cyber-console, on physical monitors under shop-floor fluorescent lighting, the pitch-black substrate creates harsh halation/glare against bright text (`#f1f5f9`), causes retinal vibration (chromostereopsis) against neon accents (`#f97316`, `#38bdf8`), and flattens surface elevation into an undifferentiated black abyss. The user's assessment is entirely accurate: transitioning to a tailored slate-charcoal / technical steel-grey foundation (`#151821` to `#1e232e`) directly aligns with high-end engineering software (GitHub, Linear, Grafana, VS Code Dark Modern) and solves both visual fatigue and surface hierarchy.

The core technical ergonomics (monospaced serials, engineering gate indicators, high-density table structure) are well-purposed for manufacturing workflows. ## Priority Issues

### [P1] Pitch-Black Obsidian Canvas Causes Eye Fatigue and Text Halation
- **Why it matters:** `#0b0f19` paired with `#f1f5f9` text creates an extreme ~16.5:1 contrast ratio. In normal or fluorescent ambient lighting, white text blooms and vibrates, causing severe eye strain over an 8-hour manufacturing shift.
- **Fix:** Shift the dark canvas from pitch-black (`#0b0f19`) to an industrial slate-charcoal grey scale: Canvas `--bg: #151821` (or `#161922`), Card/Surface `--bg2: #1c202b`, Elevated `--bg3: #242937`, Highlighting `--bg4: #2d3446`, Borders `--border: #2e3547` / `--border2: #3f475c`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] Crushed Tonal Stepping & Weak Alternating Row Visibility in Data Tables
- **Why it matters:** `#0b0f19` (Level 0) and `#111827` (Level 1) have an imperceptible 4% luminance delta. On factory IPS or standard office displays, sidebars, headers, and alternating table rows blur into a single cavernous block. Operators lose track of rows when scanning across 25+ columns.
- **Fix:** Widen the surface elevation steps: Sidenav `--sidebar-bg: #12141a`, Header `--header-bg: #181b24`, Table Row Even `--bg2: #1c202b`, Table Row Odd `--bg: #151821`, Table Hover `--row-hover-bg: #262c3b`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Chromostereopsis & Glare from Neon Accents on Pure Black
- **Why it matters:** High-saturation safety orange (`#f97316`) and cyan (`#38bdf8`) against pitch black `#0b0f19` trigger chromostereopsis (optical depth distortion where red/orange pops forward and blue recedes, vibrating on the retina).
- **Fix:** Anchor neon accents on softer tinted badge containers (e.g., subtle 10-14% alpha tints over slate grey) and slightly desaturate primary accents for resting states, reserving high-luma neon for urgent alerts.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P2] Ad-Hoc Color Proliferation and Hardcoded Dark Hexes (1,078 Detector Findings)
- **Why it matters:** Components like `Toast.jsx` (`#0d1e18`, `#231a0c`, `#241014`, `#0c1a29`), `ConfirmModal.jsx`, and inline styles hardcode arbitrary near-black hexes instead of using design tokens, leading to inconsistent color rendering and broken light/dark transitions.
- **Fix:** Replace hardcoded hexes in `Toast.jsx` and modal components with semantic theme tokens (`var(--bg2)`, `var(--border)`, `var(--text)`), eliminating palette fragmentation.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
